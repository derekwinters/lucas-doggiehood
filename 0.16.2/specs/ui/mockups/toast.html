Toast notification

A small, non-modal cream pill that slides into a reserved top-left lane to celebrate a completion — a quest finishing, an onboarding reward-chain step paying out, or a household moving in (#675) — then clears itself on a short auto-timeout (or the instant it is tapped). It mirrors the CurrencyChip's top-right reservation in the opposite corner, never dims the world, and never blocks input. One toast shows at a time; a second waits its turn.

Shown 1:1 at the true 1920×1200 reference. Two frames below show the quest and onboarding copy — no two toasts ever appear together (single slot). The move-in trigger (#675) reuses this same pill and lane unchanged; only its copy is new, listed below. The mirrored top-right chip/gear and the dashed lane guide are context only.

Regions

Coin token (leading gold coin, reuses the CurrencyChip token) · Message (one dynamic line: accomplishment + +N coins). No scrim, no button — the pill is the whole surface, and a tap anywhere on it dismisses early.

Not modal: the toast never registers with ModalInputGate and carries no backdrop — a HUD element like the currency chip, just tappable. World taps outside the lane are unaffected; a tap on the toast only dismisses it.

Dismiss — both ways: clears after ToastAutoDismissSeconds, or on tap; either runs the same ToastSlideOutMs exit. Never a trap (cf. #329).

Sequential queue (slot = 1): only one toast is visible. A second trigger firing while one shows waits and plays right after the first's slide-out — never stacked. First-come, first-served.

HUD reservation mirrors the chip: the top-left lane (dashed guide) is reserved the same way the CurrencyChip reserves top-right — nothing else renders there.
